# C803 — Copilot for Power Apps

Courseware for **Copilot for Power Apps (C803)**, a 1-day (7.5 hours), beginner,
hands-on non-WSQ short course by Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte Ltd. Learners build
business applications faster with Copilot, Microsoft's AI assistant inside Power Apps:
enabling Copilot in Power Apps Studio, preparing Dataverse data, generating a canvas
app from a prompt, refining screens and Power Fx, adding an in-app Copilot chat, then
building Power Automate approval-and-notification flows and governing the result.

Every artifact is generated from a single source (`course_data.py` + `data_domainN.py`)
so the slide deck, Lesson Plan, Learner Guide and labs stay 100% aligned.

## Course outline

1. **Get Started with Copilot in Power Apps** — what Copilot & the Power Platform are; licensing, requirements & enabling; Power Apps Studio; effective prompting; preparing Dataverse data.
2. **Build Canvas Apps with Copilot** — create an app from a prompt; refine screens, controls & layouts; generate & explain Power Fx; add an in-app Copilot chat.
3. **Automate and Extend with Copilot** — build Power Automate flows; integrate flows with canvas apps; approval & notification scenarios; share & govern AI-assisted apps.

This is a commercial non-WSQ course: **no assessment**, and it carries no SSG/SkillsFuture/WSQ
funding, TRAQOM or attendance content — each lab verifies itself with a "Test it" step.
